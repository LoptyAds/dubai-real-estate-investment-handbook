### Capital strategist vs. listing agent

Most Dubai agents are transaction-driven. They show what fits your budget, push to close, and disappear. Their incentive is velocity, your holding period or exit yield does not factor in.

A capital strategist treats a purchase as one decision inside a longer deployment plan. The question shifts from "can you afford this villa?" to "does this villa outperform the next best use of your capital over five years?" That requires understanding visa rules, rental law, service charge trends, and the actual liquidity of the asset class. One honest limitation: no advisor can guarantee rental demand or appreciation. Dubai's market cycles, and off-plan speculation has burned buyers who committed to floor plans alone. A good strategist flags where the risk sits, not just where the upside appears.
